      <h1 class="post-title">About This Journal</h1>
      <p class="post-subtitle">A personal record of walks on the Heysen Trail and across South Australia.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="post-body">
      <p>This is a simple trail journal — a place to document walks, record routes, and share film photographs from the South Australian coast and hills.</p>
      <p>All photography is shot on 35mm film. GPX tracks are recorded via Amazfit Helio and processed at home. Routes cover the Heysen Trail, the Wild South Coast Way, and surrounding public land on the Fleurieu Peninsula.</p>
      <p>Built with Jekyll and hosted on GitHub Pages. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.</p>
